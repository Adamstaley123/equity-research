▸ Methodology & data sources how every number is computed

Sources

Financial statements (revenue, EBITDA, FCF, cash, debt) come from the SEC EDGAR XBRL API — authoritative 10-Q / 10-K filings. Market data (price, shares) from Yahoo Finance. Foreign issuers without EDGAR XBRL (e.g. Adyen) fall back to a StockAnalysis source.

TTM & freshness

TTM = the four most recent quarters (or YTD reconstruction for cash-flow items). Every value is checked against the company's latest filing date; anything stale is dropped to N/A with a reason rather than shown.

Enterprise value

EV = Market Cap + Total Debt − Total Cash, computed from components. Pre-computed EV fields are not used (known stale-value issues). Market cap = price × basic shares.

EBITDA

EBITDA = Operating Income + D&A from EDGAR. Where D&A is untagged or unreliable, operating income is used as a proxy and flagged.

Estimates

Forward P/E and PEG are analyst consensus (marked EST), not filings. PEG is Yahoo's (P/E ÷ 3–5yr est. growth).

Revenue basis

Net revenue (†) keeps only the fee retained (Adyen, Marqeta, Payoneer); gross revenue includes full processing volume (Shift4, Paymentus). EV/Revenue is not directly comparable across these.

Rule of 40

Rev Growth % + FCF Margin %. For SBC-heavy names, FCF > EBITDA because stock comp is added back — EV/FCF flatters them (SBC).
